Summary
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Total cases: 10
Pass: 7  (Cases 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8)
Fail: 3  (Cases 7, 9, 10 — all on the confidence field)

Finding: the model consistently returns confidence: "high" when it is
certain about an "unclear" or off-topic classification, rather than
reflecting the strength of the customer's actual technical signal.
Root cause: prompt v1.1 lists allowed confidence values but never defines
what they mean. Fix: prompt v1.2 adds explicit confidence-tier definitions
and a rule that category-label certainty does not by itself justify "high"
confidence. See prompts/prompt_v1.2.md and
ServiceFlow_Week_2_Deliverables.docx Section 2.7 (Version History) and
Section 3 (Failures/challenges).
