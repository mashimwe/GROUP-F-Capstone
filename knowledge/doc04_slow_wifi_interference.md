Title: Slow Wi-Fi caused by channel interference or distance from the router
Category: slow_connection
Source: Team-authored, synthetic

If wired devices are fast but Wi-Fi devices are slow, the cause is often wireless interference or distance rather than the internet line itself. Thick walls, distance from the router, and interference from neighbouring Wi-Fi networks or microwave ovens on the same channel can all reduce speed. Moving closer to the router, or switching the router to the 5GHz band if available, often resolves this without any line-side fault being present.
