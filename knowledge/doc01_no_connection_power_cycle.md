Title: Power-cycling your router and modem to restore a lost connection
Category: no_connection
Source: Team-authored, based on common ISP self-help conventions (synthetic, no proprietary content)

If your internet connection is completely down, the first troubleshooting step is a full power cycle. Unplug both the modem and the router from power (not just the wall switch) and wait 30 full seconds before plugging them back in. Plug the modem in first and wait until its status lights become steady, then plug in the router and wait for it to fully boot. This clears temporary faults in the equipment's memory and resolves a large share of "no connection" reports without any further action.
