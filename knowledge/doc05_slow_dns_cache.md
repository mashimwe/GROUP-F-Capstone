Title: Slow-feeling browsing caused by DNS or browser cache issues
Category: slow_connection
Source: Team-authored, synthetic

Sometimes a connection tests as fast on a speed test but specific websites still feel slow to load. This is often a DNS resolution delay or a stale browser cache rather than a bandwidth problem. Clearing the browser cache, or trying a different browser or device on the same network, helps distinguish a true bandwidth problem from a software-side slowdown on one device.
