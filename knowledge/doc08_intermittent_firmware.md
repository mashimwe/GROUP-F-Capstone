Title: Intermittent connection caused by outdated router firmware
Category: intermittent_connection
Source: Team-authored, synthetic

Older, unpatched router firmware can cause the router to randomly restart its wireless radio or drop its connection to the modem after running for a long time. Checking the router's admin page for a firmware update, or simply restarting the router on a regular schedule, can reduce this type of intermittent fault until an update is applied.
