Title: Intermittent drops caused by a loose or damaged cable connection
Category: intermittent_connection
Source: Team-authored, synthetic

A connection that drops for a few seconds every few minutes and then reconnects on its own is frequently caused by a loose or partially damaged cable between the wall socket, modem, and router. Firmly reseat each cable end and check for any visible kinks, chew marks, or bent connectors on the cable itself. This is a common and easily fixed cause of intermittent connectivity.
