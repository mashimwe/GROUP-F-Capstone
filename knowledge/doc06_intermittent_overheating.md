Title: Intermittent drops caused by router overheating
Category: intermittent_connection
Source: Team-authored, synthetic

Routers placed in enclosed cabinets, direct sunlight, or stacked on top of other warm electronics can overheat, causing the connection to drop every so often and then recover on its own once the device cools down. Check whether the router feels hot to the touch and whether it is in an enclosed or poorly ventilated space. Moving it to an open, well-ventilated shelf is a reasonable first step before assuming a line fault.
