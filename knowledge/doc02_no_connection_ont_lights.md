Title: Reading ONT / modem status lights when there is no connection at all
Category: no_connection
Source: Team-authored, synthetic

When there is no connection at all, check the status lights on your ONT (fibre box) or modem. A solid green "Power" light with a red or blinking "LOS" (Loss of Signal) light usually means the fibre or cable line itself has a problem outside your home, not the router. A solid green "Internet" or "WAN" light with no connection on any device instead points to a router configuration issue. Note down which lights are on, off, or blinking before contacting support, since this narrows down whether the fault is on the line or in the home equipment.
