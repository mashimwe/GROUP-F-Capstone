Title: Persistent router issues caused by old or failing equipment
Category: router_modem_issue
Source: Team-authored, synthetic

If a router is more than five years old and problems persist even after power cycling, firmware updates, and a factory reset, the equipment itself may be failing. Signs include the device becoming hot very quickly, repeatedly losing its settings after a reset, or physically damaged ports. In this case, the recommended next step is equipment replacement, arranged through a support ticket rather than further remote troubleshooting.
