Title: What the router's light colours and patterns usually mean
Category: router_modem_issue
Source: Team-authored, synthetic

A steady green light generally indicates normal operation. A blinking amber or red light on the internet/WAN indicator usually means the router is not receiving a valid signal from the modem or line. A blinking red light on the Wi-Fi indicator may mean the wireless radio has crashed and needs a restart. No lights at all point to a power supply problem rather than a network fault. Note the exact colour and pattern of the light before you contact support, since it is the single most useful diagnostic clue available without special tools.
