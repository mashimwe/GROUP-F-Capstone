Title: Slow speeds caused by too many connected devices
Category: slow_connection
Source: Team-authored, synthetic

A common cause of a slow connection, especially in the evening, is simply too many devices sharing the same plan's bandwidth at once — streaming, downloads, and video calls all compete for the same capacity. Think about how many devices are typically active at the same time the slowness occurs. Turning off automatic large downloads/updates and pausing one active stream is a reasonable first check before assuming a fault, and comparing your plan's advertised speed against the number of simultaneous heavy users often explains the symptom.
