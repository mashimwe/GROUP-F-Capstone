Title: How we check whether there is an outage in your area
Category: possible_outage
Source: Team-authored, synthetic

We do not have real-time visibility into the physical network by ourselves. When you suspect an area-wide outage, we check a status tool that reflects reported incidents in your area. We will never tell you there is an outage unless that status tool confirms it. Until then we can only say that your report has been logged and that outage status is being checked.
