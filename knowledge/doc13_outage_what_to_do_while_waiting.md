Title: What to do while we confirm a possible outage
Category: possible_outage
Source: Team-authored, synthetic

While outage confirmation is pending, it is still worth power-cycling your own equipment, since a genuine local fault can look identical to an area outage from your side. If an outage is confirmed, no further action is needed from you and a restoration estimate will be shared once available; if it is not confirmed, standard equipment troubleshooting should continue.
